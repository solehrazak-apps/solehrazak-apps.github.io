Lanternlings: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Lanternlings: Times Tables is a multiplication game for children. We built it so that a child's play stays on the child's own device. This page explains exactly what that means.

What we collect

Nothing. Lanternlings does not collect, store or send any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. The app works with no internet connection.

What stays on the device

Progress (lanterns lit, Lanternlings woken, sparks, the chosen gardener), the players' first names or nicknames typed by a grown-up, and the game's settings are saved only on the device, inside the app. They never leave it. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The Isles of Twos, Fives and Tens are free. A grown-up may choose to make one optional purchase, offered only in the grown-ups' area behind a parental gate, to open every isle. The purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive any payment details.

Voice and read aloud

Pip's voice is recorded audio that comes inside the app. If a line is missing, the voice built into the device reads it instead. The app never uses the microphone, records nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

Children

Lanternlings is made for children and follows the rules of Apple's Kids category. Because we collect no information, there is nothing about any child for us to keep, share or sell.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
