Common questions

Which isles are free? The Isles of Twos, Fives and Tens. One optional purchase opens all 11 isles. There is no subscription and nothing else to buy.

How do I open the grown-ups' area? On the title screen, tap "For grown-ups", then tap the three numbers that are spelled out in words.

I bought the isles on another device. Open the grown-ups' area and tap "Restore an earlier purchase". With Family Sharing turned on, the purchase also opens the isles on your family's devices.

Can more than one child play? Yes. Up to four players each keep their own garden on the same device. Choose who is playing on the title screen.

How do I start again? Progress is kept only on the device. Deleting the app removes it.
